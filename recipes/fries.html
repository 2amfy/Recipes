Fries

Description

Crispy on the outside and soft on the inside, fries are a classic snack made from sliced potatoes fried until golden and delicious. They are often served with ketchup, sauces, or as a tasty side dish with many meals.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes - the main ingredient, preferably firm potatoes suitable for frying
  • Cooking oil - for deep frying or frying until crispy
  • Salt - for seasoning
  • Black pepper
  • Paprika or chili powder
  • Garlic powder
  • Herbs (such as parsley)
  • Cheese or sauces for toppings

Steps

  1. Wash and peel the potatoes (optional), then cut them into evenly sized strips.
  2. Rinse the potato strips in cold water to remove excess starch.
  3. Pat the potatoes completely dry with a clean towel or paper towels.
  4. Heat cooking oil to about 175-190°C.
  5. Carefully add the potatoes to the hot oil in small batches.
  6. Fry for 5-8 minutes, or until the fries are golden brown and crispy.
  7. Remove the fries with a slotted spoon and drain them on paper towels or a wire rack.
  8. Sprinkle with salt while they are still hot.
  9. Serve immediately with your favorite dipping sauce or as a side dish.
